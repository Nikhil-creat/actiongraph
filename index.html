How the agents work

Three specialists with one job each. If a model returns bad JSON, the agent retries once. If a provider fails, the other one takes over.

Extractor

Reads numbered transcript lines and returns structured JSON, including implicit tasks like “someone should look into this”.

Critic

Runs on the second model when you connect both keys. Removes items the transcript does not support, fixes owners and lowers confidence.

Planner

Writes the summary, the next steps and a ready-to-send follow-up email.

Model detection

Paste any key. “gsk_” keys go to Groq, “AIza” keys go to Gemini. The app lists the models on your account and picks the best current one.

Grounded assistant

Retrieves the most relevant transcript lines for each question, then answers with citations.

Human review

Items with no owner, no date or low confidence are flagged. Nothing is final until you tick it.

Technology
